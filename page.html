01 · The build

Built From the Frame Up.

Every Retro Ride starts as straight steel tube that we cut, bend and weld in our Jasper shop, then powder-coat twice and set on independent suspension. The fiberglass body is laid by hand and painted in PPG paint mixed to the color you pick. Nobody else gets that build.

  • Steel frame welded in our shop
  • Hand-laid fiberglass body
  • Diamond-stitched marine-grade vinyl seats
  • A VIN and a manufacturer’s certificate of origin
